# 🚗 Carpoolr

> Connect with fellow travelers and make every trip more affordable

---

## About

Carpoolr is a carpooling platform that makes it easy for people to find and share rides with others heading the same way. Whether you're a daily commuter, a student, or just need a one-time ride, Carpoolr helps you connect with drivers and passengers along your route.

# Features

- 🗺️ **Route Matching** — Find riders or drivers going your way
- 💬 **In-App Messaging** — Coordinate with your carpool group easily
- ⭐ **Ratings & Reviews** — Build trust in the community

> More features coming soon!
